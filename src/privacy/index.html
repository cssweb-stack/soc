Privacy Policy

Privacy information for this demonstration website.

This page describes the features configured in the SOC demonstration. Review and update it before launching an official department website.

Demonstration content

This site contains placeholder pages for layout and navigation testing. It has no information submission forms and no site analytics configured.

Search and external services

The search form sends your query to Google when submitted. It is scoped to this SOC demonstration’s published URL; new pages may take time to appear in search results.

The site loads Bootstrap from jsDelivr and the Inter font from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to those services when loading their resources. External links, including the X (Twitter) link, open services with their own privacy practices.

Email and questions

Email links open your email application. If you send a message, the recipient receives the information you include.

For website questions, email cssweb@hawaii.edu. See also the UH Mānoa Privacy Statement.
