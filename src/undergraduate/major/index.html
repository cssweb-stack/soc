Major (BA)

The major provides students with a broad understanding of sociology through dynamic coursework across a range of topics. Students should contact the undergraduate advisor for any questions regarding program requirements or if they are interested in majoring in sociology.

  • Learn more about our exciting major concentrations.
  • See Student Learning Outcomes.

Major (BA) Requirements for Spring 2023 and later

Students must complete 30 SOC course credits, including an introductory-level Sociology course.

1) Required Courses:

  • SOC 100 or a 200-level UH Mānoa SOC course (or equivalent)
  • SOC 300: Principles of Sociological Inquiry (4)
  • SOC 321: Survey of Sociological Theory (3)

2) Methods/Statistics Requirement (one course from):

  • SOC 475: Analysis in Survey Research (3)
  • SOC 476: Social Statistics (3) and SOC 476L: Social Statistics Laboratory (1)
  • SOC 478: Analysis in Field Research Methods (3)

3) Elective SOC Courses (to complete the 30 credit requirement)

  • 300/400-level, with at least three 400-level courses (including the Methods/Statistics course above).

On prerequisites:

  • SOC 100 or a 200-level UH Mānoa SOC course (or equivalent) is the prerequisite for 300-level SOC courses.
  • SOC 300 is the prerequisite for the 400-level SOC courses.

Additional notes:

  • All major courses must be taken for a letter grade unless they are only offered as Credit/No Credit.
  • All courses used for the major must be passed with a grade of C (not C-) or better.
  • The lower-division SOCS 225: Statistical Analysis for Social Sciences (3) may be used to fulfill the Methods/Statistics requirement, but students will still need to complete three 400-level SOC courses. However, students are encouraged to take SOC 475 or SOC 478.
  • A maximum of three credits of SOC 499: Directed Reading or Research may be counted toward the major.
  • A maximum of three credits of SOC 494/SOCS 489: Social Sciences Internship may be counted toward the major.

Major (BA) Requirements for Fall 2022 and before

Students must complete 30 upper-division (300/400-level) SOC course credits.

1) Required Courses:

  • SOC 300: Principles of Sociological Inquiry (4)
  • SOC 321: Survey of Sociological Theory (3)

2) Methods/Statistics Requirement (one course from):

  • SOC 475: Analysis in Survey Research (3)
  • SOC 476: Social Statistics (3) and SOC 476L: Social Statistics Laboratory (1)
  • SOC 478: Analysis in Field Research Methods (3)

3) Elective SOC Courses (to complete the 30-credit requirement)

  • 300/400-level, with at least three 400-level courses (including the Methods/Statistics course above).

On prerequisites:

  • SOC 100 or a 200-level UH Mānoa SOC course (or equivalent) is the prerequisite for 300-level SOC courses.
  • SOC 300 is the prerequisite for the 400-level SOC courses.

Additional notes:

  • All major courses must be taken for a letter grade unless they are only offered as Credit/No Credit.
  • Prerequisite courses and all courses used for the major must be passed with a grade of C (not C-) or better.
  • The lower-division SOCS 225: Statistical Analysis for Social Sciences (3) may be used to fulfill the Methods/Statistics requirement, but students will still need to complete three 400-level SOC courses. However, students are encouraged to take SOC 475 or SOC 478.
  • A maximum of three credits of SOC 499: Directed Reading or Research may be counted toward the major.
  • A maximum of three credits of SOC 494/SOCS 489: Social Sciences Internship may be counted toward the major.

Graduation Requirements

To graduate from UH Mānoa with a BA in Sociology, students must complete 120 credits overall, with 45 upper division (300- or 400-level) credits. A minimum of 30 credits must be taken at UH Mānoa. In addition to the total credits requirements, students must also:

  • Satisfy the Undergraduate General Education Requirements (Foundations, Diversification, Focus, and Hawaiian or Second Language)
  • Satisfy the requirements of the Sociology major.

The Sociology Program Sheet offers a comprehensive list of all of the requirements for a BA in Sociology. The requirements listed in the Sociology Program Sheet correspond to those listed under the STAR Academic Essentials tab.
