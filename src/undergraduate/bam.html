BA/MA Pathway (BAM)

The Department of Sociology’s combined BA/MA pathway (BAM) allows high-performing, highly motivated students to complete both degrees in five years. The program supports a variety of career paths and complements professional or academic training in other fields. Potential opportunities include teaching sociology at community colleges or serving as a part-time lecturer at four-year institutions.

Program features and planning considerations include:

  • The BAM program allows students to take three courses (nine credits) that count toward both the BA and the MA at the undergraduate tuition rate.
  • BAM students may choose to complete either a Plan A or a Plan B master’s degree.
  • Interested students should familiarize themselves with the Sociology BA and MA programs.
  • University of Hawaiʻi Community Colleges students should enroll in the Kaʻieʻie Program of dual enrollment with UH Mānoa.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Students must complete the gateway course, SOC 321, with a grade of B (not B-) or better before they can apply.
  • Students must take SOC 321 no later than the fall semester of their junior year.
  • Students must have a cumulative GPA of 3.0 (or higher) to apply for the BAM program.

Applications & Admissions

To apply to the BAM program, Sociology majors must meet the requirements outlined on the Applications & Admissions page and submit their applications by January 15 of their junior year for admission in the fall of the same calendar year.

  • Students accepted into the MA program receive notice at the end of the semester in which they apply.
  • The following semester, BAM students enter both programs and begin double-counted coursework at the undergraduate tuition rate.
  • Students receive a temporary faculty advisor and choose Plan A or Plan B.

Plan A: Thesis or Plan B: Research Report

Ideally the MA will be completed in BAM students' fifth year of study. Students will have completed the BA requirements in their fourth year, and they work exclusively on the remaining MA requirements in their fifth year.

  • Plan A students complete six credits of SOC 700, Thesis Research, and write and defend an MA thesis.
  • Plan B students write a research paper (rather than a thesis), take an additional theory or methods course, and take another substantive 600+ level course.

Options for Double-Counted Courses

Undergraduate Course (select one)

  • SOC 413 Economy and Society,
  • SOC 432 Punishment and Society,
  • SOC 454 Analysis in Medical Sociology,
  • SOC 456 Racism and Ethnicity in Hawaiʻi
  • SOC 459 Popular Culture

Quantitative or Qualitative Methods (select one)

  • SOC 605 Statistics for Regression Analysis (The required co-requisite course, SOC 605L, is not double-counted.)
  • SOC 606 Research Methods and Design
  • SOC 607 Seminar in Methods of Content Analysis
  • SOC 608 Survey Research Design and Analysis

Sociological Theory (select one)

  • SOC 611 Classical Sociological Theory
  • SOC 612 Contemporary Sociological Theory
