Race, Ethnicity, Gender, and Class

Research on race, ethnicity, gender, and class examines how inequalities are formed, maintained, and disrupted, with attention to the interconnections of racialized, gendered, and class relations. Areas of inquiry include demands for social justice, the justice and prison system, environmental justice, immigration, colonialism, postcolonialism, nationalism, and national formation. Topics include racism and anti-racism, racialized segregation, racial and ethnic health disparities and socioeconomic health disparities, ethnic identity formation and maintenance, ethnic boundary shift, and ethnic identity as a resource for coping with discrimination.
