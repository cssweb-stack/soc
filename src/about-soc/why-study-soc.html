Why Study SOC

Undergraduate Study

Our sociology undergraduate program offers a major (BA) and a minor.

Sociology is the study of how society organizes itself, how various groups interact with each other, and the consequences of these processes. Sociology's subject matter includes marriage and family patterns, race and ethnic relations, demography, social change, class structure, formal organizations including bureaucracies, value systems, conflict, deviant behavior, and the people and institutions of other societies.

The undergraduate program provides students with a foundation for many careers, including business and industry, community and social services, education, government, the justice system, and research.

Graduate Study

We offer two graduate programs of study: the MA in Sociology (Plan A and Plan B); and the PhD in Sociology.

The graduate program provides students with a strong foundation in the theory and methods of sociological research. Both faculty and graduate students are involved in several broad areas of sociological study, including race, ethnicity, gender, class; health, aging, medical sociology; Asia, Pacific, and Hawaiʻi in regional and global contexts; crime, law, and social control; and nationalism, globalization, and sustainability.
