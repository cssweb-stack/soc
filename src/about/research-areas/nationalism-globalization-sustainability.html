Nationalism, Globalization, and Sustainability

Research on nationalism, globalization, and sustainability considers how intensifying global connectivity and exchange unsettle conventional social arrangements and transform political, cultural, environmental, and organizational life. Subjects include nation-states and nationalism, civil society, sustainability, and the operation of politics, families, and organizations. Topics include globalization and global imaginaries, ideologies and practices of nationalism and racism, migration and anti-immigrant politics, transnational organizations and occupations, and the intersections of class, colonialism, and ideas of gender and race.
