Funding Sources

The Department of Sociology actively encourages students to pursue educational assistance. Consult the linked providers for current funding availability, eligibility, award amounts, deadlines, and application instructions.

Residency for Tuition Purposes

There is a significant difference between Hawaiʻi resident and nonresident tuition rates. Visit the Office of Admissions Hawaiʻi Residency Requirements for information on the process of acquiring resident status. Students from a number of states qualify for reduced tuition under the Western Undergraduate Exchange.

Departmental Funding Sources

  • Achievement Scholarships — Scholarships for selected Sociology majors, subject to available departmental funding.
  • Hörmann Prize — Recognizes undergraduate and graduate papers examining social and cultural phenomena in Hawaiʻi.
  • Gary Kazuo Sakihara Memorial Scholarship — Supports Sociology PhD candidates conducting dissertation research that benefits the community.
  • Funding for Professional Travel and Development — Departmental support may be available; contact the department for details. Graduate students may also explore the CSS Travel Award for Professional Development in Research, administered by SSRI (UH login required).

General Funding Resources

  • STAR (UH Foundation Scholarships) — Scholarship opportunities for students attending the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.
  • UH Scholarship Opportunities — Information about scholarships based on financial need, academic achievement, field of study, and other criteria.
  • UH Mānoa Financial Aid Services — Information about local, national, and university scholarships and financial aid.
  • International Student Services — Resources and guidance for international students.
  • SEED — Scholarship resources, including opportunities for graduate students.
  • UH Mānoa Study Abroad Center Scholarships — Scholarship information for students participating in study abroad programs.

Undergraduate Funding Opportunities

Scholarships and Student Support

  • Associated Students of the University of Hawaiʻi (ASUH) — Information about undergraduate scholarships, research awards, and academic support funding.
  • Student Support Services — Support for first-generation college students and students with financial need.
  • King David Kalakaua Scholarships — Tuition support for undergraduates pursuing studies related to the Pacific and Asia.

Research, Travel and Study Abroad

  • Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) — Supports undergraduate research, creative projects, and presentation travel.
  • Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship — Supports undergraduate study abroad.
  • United States-Japan Bridging Foundation — Scholarships for undergraduate study abroad in Japan.
  • Freeman Awards for Study in Asia (Freeman-ASIA) — Supports undergraduate study in East and Southeast Asia.
  • Udall Foundation — Undergraduate scholarships focused on leadership, public service, Native American issues, and the environment.
  • Wakaba Kai Alumni Association Endowed Scholarship — Supports undergraduates with academic or service interests in Japanese studies and culture.

Paper Awards

  • Alpha Kappa Delta — Recognizes undergraduate sociology papers through a student paper competition.
  • American Society of Criminology Division of Women and Crime — Recognizes undergraduate research through student paper and poster awards.
  • Sociology of Law Section of the American Sociological Association — Recognizes undergraduate student papers in the sociology of law.

Graduate Funding Opportunities

Graduate research and teaching assistantships may be available through university units or individual faculty members. See Career Opportunities at the University of Hawaiʻi (NeoGov) for openings and position-specific information about qualifications, funding, and benefits.

Student Employment

Undergraduate and graduate students can explore campus employment through Student Employment and Cooperative Education. Consult individual job listings for duties, eligibility, and application instructions.

Specialized Funded Programs Affiliated with UH

  • Mānoa Political Internships — Internship opportunities combining government experience and academic study.
  • Foreign Language and Area Studies (FLAS) Fellowships — Funding for Asian language and area studies. Explore opportunities through East Asian studies and Southeast Asian studies.
  • Boren Fellowship and Scholarship — Funding opportunities for overseas study through the National Security Education Program.
  • Hawaiʻi Community Foundation Scholarships — Information about scholarship opportunities through the Hawaiʻi Community Foundation.
  • Ehime Prefectural Summer Internship (EPIC) — Summer internship opportunities in Japan that foster connections between Ehime and Hawaiʻi.
  • Japan-America Student Conference (JASC) Scholarship — Supports undergraduate and graduate participation in the Japan-America Student Conference.
