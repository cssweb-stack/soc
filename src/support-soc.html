Support SOC

The Na HoʻAloha O Sociology Fund (Friends of Sociology Fund) provides general support for the programs and events of the Department of Sociology, including enabling the department to offer honoraria to visiting speakers and to co-host special conferences and events. This fund also serves as the Aloha Fund of the department when it is appropriate to recognize visitors and or to celebrate special accomplishments of members of the department. Contributions of any size are sincerely appreciated.
