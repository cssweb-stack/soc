SOC: Understanding Society, Inspiring Change

E komo mai — Welcome to the Department of Sociology within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. Sociology opens new ways of understanding society, exploring what shapes it, and discovering how it changes. Sociology connects questions about everyday life with the larger forces shaping society. Students and faculty investigate social relationships, institutions, and inequalities, with particular attention to social justice. Grounded in Hawaiʻi and connected to Asia, the Pacific, and beyond, the department brings different perspectives and research approaches to understanding an interconnected world.

Research Areas

Faculty and students investigate social relationships, institutions, and change through five areas of research and teaching.

Asia, Pacific, and Hawaiʻi in Regional and Global Contexts Crime, Law, and Social Control Health, Aging, and Medical Sociology Nationalism, Globalization, and Sustainability Race, Ethnicity, Gender, and Class

Academic Programs

From foundational study to advanced research, students explore society and the forces shaping it.
