MA Example Progress

This is one possible sequence, not a required schedule.

  • Students are strongly encouraged to complete Theory, Methods, and Statistics courses by the end of their first year, along with any Substantive courses directly relevant to their intended thesis topic.
  • Thesis proposals should be completed in the third semester at the latest so that sufficient time remains for thesis writing.
