PhD Suggested Timeline

The example below shows one way to complete the Sociology PhD program in six years, including coursework, examinations, and dissertation milestones.

PhD Example Progress

This sequence represents one possible plan which will satisfy PhD program requirements in six years.

  • This is a conservative plan. Students may be able to complete the program more quickly, depending on their outside commitments, and should consult with their advisor when planning their timeline.
  • Required courses are typically offered in the same semester year after year, but there may be exceptions that force adjustments to this template.
  • The Graduate Division time limit for completion of the degree is seven years.
  • Substantive courses listed here are at the 600 level or higher, as are the additional methods or statistics courses.
  • Candidates who come in without prior coursework in social theory and/or statistics need to take SOC 321 prior to taking SOC 611/612 and/or SOC 476/L prior to taking SOC605/L.
  • Time for preparation of a dissertation will depend in part on the nature of the topic and research methodology.
